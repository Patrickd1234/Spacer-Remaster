# monday.com (work management, monday dev, platform): product profile as of November 2026

Research date: 2026-11-19. Source-quality notes: monday.com's pricing page and SEC filing are primary. G2 Learn is editorial content from a review platform. TinyCommand, Activepieces, Kroolo, Cotera and ClickUp are competitors with an incentive to criticize monday.com. till-freitag.com is a monday.com partner/consultancy. Capterra and G2 review pages returned HTTP 403 to direct fetch, so the review aggregates below come from search snippets and G2's own editorial review.

## 1. Core features: boards/views, columns, automations, integrations, dashboards, forms, docs, guests, templates, time tracking, mobile, monday dev, AI, API/marketplace

### Takeaway
monday.com is a configurable "Work OS" built around boards made of items and typed columns, with multiple views (table, kanban, timeline/Gantt, calendar, workload), dashboards, forms (WorkForms), Docs, automations and integrations. Many of the views that separate tiers are gated: Timeline, Gantt and Calendar start on Standard, time tracking and private boards start on Pro, and portfolio and resource management are Enterprise-only. In 2025-2026 the company repositioned as an "AI Work Platform" (rebuild announced May 7, 2026). Its main AI products are AI agents, Sidekick (assistant), Vibe (prompt-to-app), Magic, AI Blocks and an MCP server, all metered by AI credits. monday dev adds scrum/kanban sprints, roadmaps, burndown/velocity charts and GitHub/GitLab/Bitbucket/Azure DevOps/Jira integrations.

### Inferences
- The tier gating shows what monday treats as premium: timeline/Gantt/calendar views and guests (Standard), private boards, time tracking and formula columns (Pro), and portfolio/resource management (Enterprise). A smaller app could include these on its base tier as a differentiator.
- AI is becoming a metered second axis of pricing on top of seats. Free AI conveniences (formula builder, prompt-to-board) are now table stakes. Agentic actions are where monday charges.
- monday dev's breadth claims are wide (integrations with AI IDEs like Cursor and Windsurf). Third-party reviewers still say it is shallower than Jira at the issue level (see section 4).

### Gaps
- No official count of column types on paid plans (only "8 column types" on Free). I didn't verify the commonly cited "30+ column types" claim.
- No primary source confirms which tier unlocks Workload view, or a Standard guest ratio.
- No primary documentation was fetched on the GraphQL API (developer.monday.com API reference) or on API complexity limits.
- I found no official 2026 AI Feature Catalog page. Credit costs come from a partner blog.
- Dashboard board-combination limits for work management (as opposed to dev and CRM) were not shown on the captured pricing page. G2 says Standard combines up to 5 boards.

## 2. Pricing tiers in 2026, seat minimums and buckets, automation/integration limits, notable changes

### Takeaway
Work management costs $0 (Free, up to 2 seats), $9 (Basic), $12 (Standard), $19 (Pro) per seat per month billed annually, or custom (Enterprise). Paid plans have a 3-seat minimum and are sold in seat buckets, so a team of 6 pays for 10. Monthly billing is not discounted. Automation and integration actions are capped at 250/month (Standard), 25,000/month (Pro) and 250,000/month combined (Enterprise). The biggest 2026 change is not seat prices but a "seats + AI credits" model, launched May 6, 2026 and extended to Sidekick and AI workflow runs on July 20, 2026. Seat prices appear unchanged from the 2024-2025 levels.

### Cited Findings
**Work management tiers (annual, per seat/month)**
- Free $0 (up to 2 seats); Basic $9; Standard $12; Pro $19; Enterprise custom. Example totals at 10 seats: $90 / $120 / $190 — [monday.com pricing](https://monday.com/pricing)
- Monthly AI credits by tier: Basic 1,000, Standard 2,000, Pro 3,000, Enterprise custom — [monday.com pricing](https://monday.com/pricing)
- Automations per month: Standard 250, Pro 25,000. Integrations per month: Standard 250, Pro 25,000. Enterprise: 250,000 automations and integrations combined — [monday.com pricing](https://monday.com/pricing)
- FAQ: "Plans start from 3 users." "If you want to sign up more than 40 users, you can request a quote." "The monthly plan is not discounted." Payment is "made in one upfront installment." Yearly plans are refundable within 30 days. A 14-day Pro trial is available. Prices exclude tax and vary by country — [monday.com pricing](https://monday.com/pricing)
- Same prices in Jan 2026: Standard $12 and Pro $19, each with a 3-user minimum, billed annually in USD — [G2 Learn review](https://learn.g2.com/monday-review)

**Other products (annual, per seat/month)**
- monday dev: Basic $9, Standard $12, Pro $20, Enterprise custom. Automations/integrations: Standard 250/250, Pro 25K/25K, Enterprise 250K/250K. Enterprise includes a 99.9% uptime SLA and a dedicated CSM — [monday.com pricing](https://monday.com/pricing)
- monday CRM: Basic $12 ($18 monthly), Standard $17 ($25), Pro $28 ($41), Ultimate custom ("a new name for our Enterprise tier") — [monday.com pricing](https://monday.com/pricing)
- monday service: Standard $31, Pro $45, Enterprise custom — [monday.com pricing](https://monday.com/pricing)

**Seat buckets**
- Paid seats "sell in steps of 3, 5, 10, 15 and up," so a team of six pays for ten. The rounding is "invisible on the pricing page and very visible on the invoice" — [TinyCommand, updated Sept 15 2026 (competitor)](https://tinycommand.com/blogs/monday-pricing-explained)
- A CRM pricing guide lists buckets of roughly 3, 5, 10, 15, 20, 25, 30, 40, 50+. Another source says "the 31st person moves a bill from 30 to 40 seats" — [saascrmreview](https://saascrmreview.com/monday-com-pricing/); [Kroolo "ghost seat tax" (competitor)](https://kroolo.com/blog/mondaycoms-ghost-seat-tax-explained). These are third-party sources, and I couldn't read the official support page ([support.monday.com 4405633151634](https://support.monday.com/hc/en-us/articles/4405633151634-How-does-plan-and-product-pricing-work-)) in fetched content.
- G2's own editor calls the model a "seat bucket" structure that "requires math to budget" — [G2 Learn review](https://learn.g2.com/monday-review)

**What happens at automation limits**
- When the cap is hit, active automations stop until the next billing cycle. The remedy described is upgrading the whole account — [Activepieces (competitor), Nov 13 2026](https://www.activepieces.com/blog/monday-com-pricing-2026-plans-costs-automation-limits). The same article contradicts itself on seat-rounding math, so use with caution.
- Busy boards with notifications and syncs "burn through" low caps faster than teams expect — [TinyCommand](https://tinycommand.com/blogs/monday-pricing-explained). One anecdote reports a team exhausting Standard's limit in 10 days (secondary, no primary thread found) — via search summary of [automationatlas.io](https://automationatlas.io/answers/monday-com-review-2026/)
- Limits apply per account, not per user (community thread title) — [monday community](https://community.monday.com/t/limit-of-automation-integration-is-per-account-or-per-user/92937)

**2025-2026 pricing changes**
- May 2026: a seats-plus-credits model launched with the AI Work Platform, announced with Q1 2026 results. Bain classifies it as "hybrid" (usage-based AI meters on top of seats) — [UC Today, May 12 2026](https://www.uctoday.com/project-management/is-per-seat-saas-pricing-dead-monday-coms-consumption-based-pricing-pivot/); [Subscription Insider](https://www.subscriptioninsider.com/article-type/news/monday-com-links-ai-pricing-to-seats-and-credits-in-q1-results). Secondary sources say the model applies to customers joining from May 6, 2026 and that seat prices did not change — [usagepricing.com May 6 2026 packaging](https://usagepricing.com/blueprint/activity/monday-com-2026-05-06-packaging)
- Q4 2025: monthly free AI credits were replaced by a one-time trial balance (12,000 credits Enterprise, 6,000 other tiers). July 20, 2026: credits extended to Sidekick, Sidekick image generation and AI workflow runs. Credit packs: $960/yr for 8,000, $2,400/yr for 20,000; monthly packs cost about 25% more — [till-freitag](https://till-freitag.com/en/blog/monday-ai-features-en). **Conflict:** the live pricing page now lists monthly AI credit allowances per tier (1,000/2,000/3,000) — [monday.com pricing](https://monday.com/pricing). Allowances may have been reinstated after the trial-balance change.
- Feb 2026: a reported 18% price increase on monday service tiers at renewal (secondary, unverified) — [usagepricing.com Feb 10 2026](https://usagepricing.com/blueprint/activity/monday-com-2026-02-10-price-change)
- **Spurious data warning:** CostBench logs Feb 2026 "increases" from $9 to $90 and $29 to $190. These are artifacts of the pricing page showing 10-seat totals, and CostBench itself says they are page-capture diffs, not confirmed changes — [CostBench](https://www.costbench.com/changelog/monday-price-increase-2026-02/)

### Inferences
- Effective entry price for a 4-6 person team on Standard is about $60-120/month annually, because of the 3-seat minimum and bucket rounding. A per-actual-seat or flat-team price is a clear wedge for a smaller competitor.
- Standard's 250 actions/month is very low (about 8/day for the whole account). Real automation use effectively requires Pro at $19/seat. "Generous automations on the base plan" is a credible benchmark claim.
- AI credits add a second, less predictable cost axis. Expect "bill shock" complaints to migrate from seats to credits.

### Gaps
- I couldn't access the official bucket ladder (support article content not returned). The exact buckets above 50 are unverified.
- Monthly-billing per-seat prices for work management were not in the captured pricing content.
- No primary source on whether extra automation actions can be bought as an add-on without upgrading.
- The Bridge "why it keeps changing" article was not fetched.

## 3. Target users, positioning, onboarding and ease-of-use reputation

### Takeaway
monday.com sells to non-technical and cross-functional business teams (marketing, ops, HR, PMO, light CRM) with a highly visual, configurable product rated strongly for ease of use. It is moving upmarket: growth is concentrated in $50K+ and $100K+ ARR accounts, and the 2026 positioning is an "AI work platform where people and agents work side by side." Overall growth is slowing, and a 2026 restructuring is underway.

### Cited Findings
- Best fit per G2's editor: marketing and creative teams, SMBs, cross-functional project teams, remote/hybrid teams, and non-technical departments (HR, event planning, light CRM). Less suited to strict, developer-centric workflows, where it points to Jira — [G2 Learn review](https://learn.g2.com/monday-review)
- G2 customer mix: small business 63%, mid-market 29%, enterprise 8%. Ease of use 92%, ease of setup 89%, user adoption 75%, time to ROI about 11 months. Rated 4.7/5 from nearly 15,000 users — [G2 Learn review](https://learn.g2.com/monday-review)
- G2's AI Work Platform listing shows 4.7/5 from 16,942 reviews. Capterra shows 4.6 from 6,037 ratings, with ease of use 4.5 and customer service 4.4 (search-snippet data; direct fetch blocked) — [Capterra reviews](https://www.capterra.com/p/147657/monday-com/reviews/?page=2); [G2 survey response page](https://www.g2.com/survey_responses/monday-ai-work-platform-review-3603798)
- Positioning in 2026: rebuilt as an AI work platform for marketing, sales, HR, support, procurement and operations. "monday.com is now a place where people and agents work side by side" (co-CEO Roy Mann). More than 250,000 customers — [IT Brief UK, May 7 2026](https://itbrief.co.uk/story/monday-com-rebuilds-platform-around-ai-work-agents)
- Cotera argues monday is easy for non-technical stakeholders: "a marketing manager can open Monday and understand what's happening without a Jira tutorial" — [Cotera, Mar 2026](https://cotera.co/articles/jira-vs-monday-comparison.md)

**Company metrics (Q3 2026, reported Nov 9 2026)**

### Inferences
- Customer count is growing slowly (+5% for >10-user accounts) while large accounts grow fast. monday is optimizing for enterprise expansion and AI monetization, not small teams. That leaves space for a simpler, cheaper app aimed at small teams, the segment the seat minimum penalizes.

## 4. Common complaints: seat buckets, automation limits, software-team depth, big-board performance, notification noise

### Takeaway
Across Capterra and G2, the dominant complaint is pricing: expensive, confusing, seat minimums and buckets, and features gated behind tiers. Performance with large or automation-heavy boards and the learning curve or feature overload come next. Notification noise is a recurring secondary complaint. Third parties consistently say monday dev lacks Jira-level depth for engineering teams.

### Cited Findings
**Pricing and seats**
- Capterra theme "High and confusing pricing structure": 54% negative across 828 tagged reviews. G2 tag "Expensive": 527 reviews. One reviewer says per-user charging gets expensive compared to alternatives — search-snippet summary of [Capterra reviews](https://www.capterra.com/p/147657/monday-com/reviews/?page=17) and [G2](https://www.g2.com/survey_responses/monday-ai-work-platform-review-3603798) (direct fetch blocked, so treat the exact percentages as snippet-level)
- The seat model "requires math to budget," and lower tiers cap actions so teams "hit limits quickly" and need to upgrade — [G2 Learn review](https://learn.g2.com/monday-review)
- The seat minimum penalizes solo users and very small teams, making monday one of the more expensive options per user at the low end — search summary of [Kroolo (competitor)](https://kroolo.com/blog/is-there-a-project-management-tool-with-no-seat-minimum) and [TinyCommand (competitor)](https://tinycommand.com/blogs/monday-pricing-explained)

**Automation limits**
- Advanced automations are gated behind higher tiers, and Standard's caps are hit quickly — [G2 Learn review](https://learn.g2.com/monday-review)
- Automations stop until the next cycle once the cap is reached — [Activepieces (competitor)](https://www.activepieces.com/blog/monday-com-pricing-2026-plans-costs-automation-limits)

**Performance**
- Capterra theme "occasional slow performance issues": 67% negative across 187 tagged reviews. G2 reviewers report slowness with large datasets, and board load times slow when many boards and automations are active — search-snippet summary of [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/147657/monday-com/reviews/?page=2)
- Occasional UI and data-refresh problems when working across many boards — [ProofHub review (competitor)](https://www.proofhub.com/articles/monday-review)

**Notifications**
- Users involved in many tasks get heavy notification volume — [G2 Learn review](https://learn.g2.com/monday-review)
- A G2 reviewer says they can't manage notifications from updates. A Capterra reviewer wants more scheduling options for reminders and notifications — search-snippet summary of [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/147657/monday-com/reviews/?page=2)

**Learning curve and feature overload**
- G2 tag "Overwhelming Features": 503 reviews — search snippet via [G2](https://www.g2.com/survey_responses/monday-ai-work-platform-review-3603798)
- Features, templates and customization overwhelm new users. Setup needs upfront planning, and boards get overbuilt — [G2 Learn review](https://learn.g2.com/monday-review)

**Depth for software teams**
- Jira is "often a better fit" for engineering-heavy teams that need strict agile frameworks and issue-level depth — [G2 Learn review](https://learn.g2.com/monday-review)
- "Monday works for PMOs and falls apart for dev teams" — [Cotera, Mar 2026](https://cotera.co/articles/jira-vs-monday-comparison.md)
- monday dev is seen as oriented to non-engineering users: Jira excels at connecting development tools — [thectoclub monday dev vs Jira](https://thectoclub.com/tools/monday-dev-vs-jira/) (templated comparison site)

**Mobile and UI**
- Mobile is limited to quick updates. Kanban sub-items need extra clicks — [G2 Learn review](https://learn.g2.com/monday-review)

### Inferences
- The most exploitable weaknesses for a smaller competitor are: (1) transparent pricing for the actual number of seats, with no 3-seat minimum or buckets; (2) usable automation volume on the entry tier; (3) fast performance on large boards; and (4) sane notification defaults with digest and batching controls.
- Software-team depth (hierarchy, issue linking, workflow strictness) is monday's weak flank against Jira. A smaller app targeting mixed technical and non-technical teams can position between the two.
